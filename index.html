Questions

Quick answers before you order.

Do you need my password?

No. We only need your numeric UID. Never share your password or OTP with anyone.

How long does delivery take?

We deliver after we confirm your payment. Timing depends on how busy we are, and we’ll message you when your order is done.

I entered the wrong UID. What now?

Message us before paying. Once diamonds are sent to a UID, we can’t move them to another account.

Can I get a refund?

If we can’t complete your order, you get your money back. Contact us with your order ID.
